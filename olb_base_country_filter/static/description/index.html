Screens per Country

The fields of a localization only show in the companies of its country. A free technical module used by our localization modules: in a database with companies in several countries, each one sees only what applies to it.

Why teams use it

Per country of the company

The fields declared for a country only show while the active company belongs to it.

Forms, lists and kanban

Fields are hidden in forms and kanban cards, and their columns removed from lists.

Follows the company switcher

Switching the active company shows the screens of the new company's country.

One call for developers

A module lists its fields and countries in _get_view; the views are cached per company.

FAQ

Does it change anything on its own?
No. It only hides the fields that other modules declare for a country.
Do I have to install it?
It comes with the modules that need it. You can also install it alone to build your own.
Is it really free?
Yes, free and LGPL-3.
