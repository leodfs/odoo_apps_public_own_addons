POS Translation Fix

Some point of sale texts of Odoo 18.0 stay in English whatever the language of the cashier. This free module makes them translatable and ships their Spanish translation. It installs by itself with the point of sale and changes nothing else.

See it in action

Real screens from Odoo 18.0, point of sale in Spanish.

01 · NUMBER WINDOWS

Confirmar and Descartar

The windows that ask for a number (employee PIN, discount, price, quantity, guests, tip) say Confirmar and Descartar, not Ok and Discard.

02 · ORDERS

The list of orders, in Spanish

Searching for an order that does not exist says No se encontraron órdenes, not No orders found.

03 · CASH IN AND OUT

Salida de efectivo, not out

The notification, the receipt, the note of the session and the label of the cash move name the move in the language of the cashier.

04 · RECEIPT

The e-mail field

The example address of the e-mail field of the receipt screen is translated too.

05 · ORDERS

Seleccione una orden

Before choosing an order, the list says it in Spanish, without quotes.

06 · ACTIONS

Acciones

The window of the Actions button has its title in Spanish, not Actions.

FAQ

Does it change how the point of sale works?
No. It only makes texts translatable.
I installed it and still see English.
Reload the point of sale in the browser of each till: it keeps its files in cache.
Can you add my language?
Yes, on request. The texts are now translatable like any other.
Is there a user manual?
Yes, a short PDF manual in English and Spanish is included in the module.
